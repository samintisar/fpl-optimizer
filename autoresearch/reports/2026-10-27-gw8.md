What the diagnostics found (in `~/fpl-research/results/research/`):
- `diag-prior-bands.txt`: the MID goal-share price prior is concave in every fit season:
  ≤ £5.0m 0.69–0.89 of the log-linear prior, £5.5–8.0m 1.14–1.60, ≥ £9.5m 0.71–0.85, with
  assists alike. DEF and FWD are flat. This was the second keep.
- `diag-price-curve.txt`: the finishing curves are mostly concave for MID and convex for FWD
  in 2020 and 2022. DEF is unstable: in 2018 c = −0.065, which gives a par of 0.43 at £10.0m.
  The gain comes from taming the extrapolation at the price extremes. **Promotion check
  below.**
- `diag-goals-fpl.txt`, `diag-fwd-pens.txt`, `diag-fwd-finish.txt`: after both keeps, FWD
  starters' goals are still over-predicted (FPL goals 0.936; 2020–22 open play 0.907, MID
  1.058; penalties fine). The cause is **FWD own finishing being over-trusted**. By last
  season's np goals − npxG, FWDs score 0.70–0.72 of expected after +1 to +3 or more, and 1.05
  after ≤ −3. Understat's `us_npg` is incomplete before 2019/20, so the earlier Understat-only
  FWD numbers were partly coverage.
- `diag-low-minutes.txt`: develop P(start) is calibrated by band. The low-band minutes
  excess comes from flat conditional minutes, and the variant that fixes it moves little in
  points.
- `diag-defcon-dev.txt`: the defcon count model (in-season rate, `DEFCON_K`, NB `DEFCON_R`)
  given the realized minutes is calibrated on 2016/17–2018/19. Examples: DEF band 0.3–0.4
  0.348 vs 0.361, implied r 12.7 vs 13.2.
